Today's Namaz Timings — Karachi

Aaj ki namaz ke auqaat — select your city, phir apna maslak chunein: Ahl-e-Sunnat, Deoband ya Ahl-e-Hadith.

Asal farq sirf Asr me hai: Ahl-e-Sunnat aur Deoband dono Hanafi hain (Misal Doem — saya 2 guna), is liye in ki tamam timings ek hi hain. Ahl-e-Hadith Misal Awwal par hai (saya 1 guna), is liye sirf Asr pehle hoti hai — Fajr, Dhuhr, Maghrib aur Isha teeno me ek hi hain (Karachi method 18°/18°).

Method: University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi — Fajr & Isha at 18°. Times are calculated on this page (no external API), so they stay free and fast. Small differences from your local masjid timetable (1–3 minutes) are normal — method and rounding differ.
