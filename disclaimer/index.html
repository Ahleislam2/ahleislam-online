Calculators and timings

Zakat calculator sirf andaza batata hai — wo aapke diye hue dar (rate), raqam aur zimmedariyon par mabni hai. Sone/chandi ka dar roz badalta hai, is liye hisab karte waqt maujooda dar khud darj karein aur hatmi adaigi se pehle apne aalim se tasdeeq kar lein.

Namaz timings isi page par calculate hoti hain (Karachi method, 18°/18°) — apni masjid ke timetable se 1–3 minute ka farq normal hai. Namaz ke liye apni masjid ke auqaat ko tarjeeh dein.

Hijri tareekhein tabular (hisabi) hain — maqami chand dekhne ki tareekh se taqreeban 1 din ka farq ho sakta hai. Roza, Eid ya kisi tehwar ki hatmi tareekh ke liye apne ilaqe ke elan par amal karein.
