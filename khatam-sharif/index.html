Khatam Sharif — Mukammal Guide

Khatam Sharif ka matlab hai: Quran ki tilawat, zikr aur Durood parh kar us ka sawab kisi marhoom (ya zinda) ko pohanchana — is amal ko Isal-e-Sawab kehte hain. Is page par do tareeqe hain: (1) ghar par parha jane wala saada Khatam, jo Pakistan me sab se zyada raaij hai, aur (2) Naqshbandi Khatm-e-Khwajgan ka lamba aur chhota version.

Share this guide
Imandari wali baat (pehle parh lein): Khatam ki ye makhsoos tadad (kitni dafa kya parhna hai) Quran ya Hadith me muqarrar nahi hai — ye ulama aur silsilon ki riwayat se chali aa rahi tartib hai. Asal daleel sirf is baat ki hai ke tilawat, dua aur sadqe ka sawab marhoom ko pohancha ja sakta hai — wo daleel neeche Section 3 me hai. Apne ghar ya masjid ki raaij tartib par amal karna bhi theek hai.

1. Saada Khatam (ghar wala tareeqa)

Ye 4 steps hain — shuru aur aakhir Durood Shareef se, darmiyan me Quran ki tilawat:

Step 1 — shuru me

Durood Shareef

اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَّعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ

Allahumma salli ‘ala Muhammadin wa ‘ala aali Muhammad.

Ya Allah, Muhammad ﷺ par aur un ki aal par rehmat nazil farma.

Step 2

4 Qul — Surah Kafirun, Ikhlas (3 dafa), Falaq, Nas

Surah Ikhlas 3 dafa parhi jati hai, baqi teen Surahen 1-1 dafa:

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ﴿١﴾ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ﴿٢﴾ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ﴿٣﴾ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ ﴿٤﴾

Qul huwAllahu Ahad. Allahus Samad. Lam yalid wa lam yulad. Wa lam yakul lahu kufuwan Ahad.

Keh do: Allah aik hai. Allah be-niyaz hai. Na us ne kisi ko jana, na wo jana gaya. Aur koi us ka humsar nahi. (Surah Ikhlas, 112:1-4)

Surah Kafirun (109), Surah Falaq (113) aur Surah Nas (114) bhi isi tarah parhein — Arabic text Tanzil se, tarjuma agle Surah pages par aa raha hai.

Step 3

Surah Fatiha + Surah Baqarah ki pehli 5 aayaten

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ﴿١﴾ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ﴿٢﴾ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ﴿٣﴾ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ﴿٤﴾ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ﴿٥﴾ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ﴿٦﴾ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ ﴿٧﴾

Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem. Alhamdu lillahi Rabbil ‘alameen. Ar-Rahmanir Raheem. Maliki yawmid-deen. Iyyaka na‘budu wa iyyaka nasta‘een. Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqeem. Siratal-lazeena an‘amta ‘alayhim, ghayril-maghdoobi ‘alayhim wa lad-daalleen.

Allah ke naam se jo bara meherban, nihayat rehem wala hai. Sab tareef Allah ke liye hai jo tamam jahanon ka Rabb hai. (Surah Fatiha, 1:1-7 — Arabic text: Tanzil, CC-BY 3.0)

Phir Surah Baqarah ki ibtedai aayaten (2:1-5) parhein — “Alif Lam Meem, ye wo kitab hai jis me koi shak nahi, parhezgaron ke liye hidayat hai.”

Step 4 — aakhir me

Durood Shareef (dobara)

Durood se shuru, Durood par khatam — ye adab hai ke dua Durood ke darmiyan qabooliyat ke zyada qareeb hoti hai.

2. Isal-e-Sawab ki Dua (sawab pohanchane ki dua)

Tilawat ke baad haath utha kar dua karein — apne alfaz me bhi jaiz hai, ye aik namuna hai:

“Ya Allah! Jo kuch hum ne parha hai — Quran ki tilawat, zikr aur Durood — us ka sawab sab se pehle hamare Nabi Muhammad ﷺ ko, phir tamam Anbiya, Sahaba, Ahl-e-Bayt aur Awliya ko, aur phir mere marhoomeen (naam lein) ko pohancha de. Un ki maghfirat farma, un ke darjaat buland farma, aur hamein bhi apni rehmat se nawaz de. Aameen.”

Is ke baad apni zaati duaein (sehat, rizq, mushkil) maang sakte hain — phir aakhir me aik dafa Surah Fatiha parh lein.

3. Daleel — kya sawab pohancha ja sakta hai?

Quran — waliden ke liye dua

“Aye mere Rabb! Mere waliden par rehem farma, jaisa unhon ne bachpan me meri parwarish ki.” (Surah Bani Israil, 17:24) — waliden ke liye dua ka hukam khud Quran me hai.

Quran — guzar jane walon ke liye dua

“Aye Allah! Hamein bakhsh de aur hamare un bhaiyon ko jo iman la kar hum se pehle guzar gaye.” (Surah Hashr, 59:10) — guzar jane walon ke liye maghfirat ki dua ki tareef ki gayi hai.

Hadith — marhoom ki taraf se sadqa

Sahih Bukhari aur Sahih Muslim me hai ke aik shakhs ne poocha: meri walida achanak wafat pa gayi hain, agar un ki taraf se sadqa dun to kya un ko sawab milega? Nabi ﷺ ne farmaya: “Haan.” Isi tarah Bukhari ki riwayat me hai ke marhoom ka darja is liye buland hota hai ke us ka beta us ke liye maghfirat ki dua karta hai.

Ulama ka mauqif (khulasa): Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn Qayyim aur Qadi Shawkani jaise ulama ne likha hai ke dua, tilawat, Hajj aur sadqe ka sawab marhoom ko pohancha ja sakta hai — ye Ahle-Sunnat ka mashhoor mauqif hai.

4. Naqshbandi Khatm-e-Khwajgan (lamba + chhota)

Ye Naqshbandi silsile ka ijtemai zikr hai, riwaj ke mutabiq Jumerat ki shaam ko parha jata hai. Chhote version me sirf “Quran ki tilawat wala hissa” mukhtasar hota hai, baqi sab tadad wahi rehti hai:

    Ye tartib Naqshbandi riwayat ke mutabiq bayan ki gayi hai; mukammal Arabi matan aur tafseel ke liye apne silsile ki kitaab ya masjid ke imam se ruju karein.

    Aam Sawal (FAQs)

    Kya Khatam sirf Jumerat ko hi ho sakta hai?

    Nahi. Jumerat ki shaam riwaj hai (is liye ke log jama ho sakte hain), lekin Isal-e-Sawab kisi bhi din, kisi bhi waqt ho sakta hai — din fix karna sirf sahulat hai, shart nahi.

    Kya tadad (jaise Ikhlas 3 dafa) lazmi hai?

    Nahi — ye riwaj ki tartib hai, Quran/Hadith ka muqarrar hukam nahi. Kam ya zyada parhna bhi jaiz hai; asal cheez ikhlas aur dua hai.

    Kya aurat ghar par Khatam kar sakti hai?

    Ji haan. Tilawat, zikr aur dua ghar par bhi ho sakti hai — kisi khaas jagah ya majlis ki shart nahi.

    Kya Khatam ke liye khana khilana zaroori hai?

    Nahi. Khana khilana (niyaz) aik alag sadqa hai — achha amal hai, lekin Khatam ki shart nahi. Khatam sirf tilawat + dua se bhi mukammal hai.
