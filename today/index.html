Har Mahine — Khatam ke Din

Chatti Sharif — har mahine ki 6 tareekh: Khwaja Garib Nawaz ki yaad me — Quran, Zikr, Fatiha, Isal-e-Sawab.
Gyarvi Sharif — har mahine ki 11 tareekh: Ghaus-e-Azam ki yaad me — Quran, Zikr, Fatiha, Isal-e-Sawab.
Saalana bara Urs: Chatti Sharif 6 Rajab (Ajmer Sharif), Gyarvi Sharif 11 Rabi al-Thani (Baghdad Sharif).
