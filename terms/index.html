Accuracy limits — please read

Namaz timings isi page par calculate hoti hain (Karachi method, 18°/18°). Apni masjid ke timetable se 1–3 minute ka farq normal hai — namaz ke liye apni masjid ke auqaat ko tarjeeh dein.

Hijri tareekhein tabular (hisabi) hain — maqami chand dekhne ki tareekh se taqreeban 1 din ka farq ho sakta hai.

Zakat calculator sirf aapke diye hue dar (rate) aur raqam par hisab karta hai — asli nisab aur zakat ki adaigi ke liye apne aalim se mashwara karein.

Method & honesty
