About Ahl-e-Islam

Ahl-e-Islam (ahleislam.online) ek free Islamic tools aur learning site hai — rozana ki zaroorat ki cheezein ek hi jagah, saaf design me, hawalon ke sath. Is site ko use karne ke liye koi account, login ya sign-up nahi chahiye.

What you will find here

Namaz timings — shehar ke hisab se namaz ke auqaat, teen maslak ke sath (Ahl-e-Sunnat, Deoband, Ahl-e-Hadith).

Zakat calculator — cash, sona/chandi, tijarti maal aur zimmedariyon ke hisab se zakat ka andaza, user ke apne dar (rate) par.

Islamic Calendar — aaj ki Hijri tareekh, Hijri jantri aur Gregorian se Hijri me tabdeeli.

Quran Pak — tamam 114 Surahain, Arabic ke sath Urdu aur English tarjuma.

Hadith — mashhoor ahadith, kitab aur number ke hawale ke sath.

Khatam Sharif — Khatam Sharif ka mukammal guide.

Islamic Quiz — seekhne ke liye sawal-jawab, har roz naye sawal.

Who is this site for?

Har us shakhs ke liye jo rozana namaz ke auqaat, Hijri tareekh, zakat ka hisab, Quran ki tilawat aur tarjuma, ya mustanad ahadith dekhna chahta hai — chahe wo talib-e-ilm ho, ghar ka fard ho, ya koi bhi musalman jo apne deen ko behtar samajhna chahta hai.

Sources and method — stated plainly

Quran ka Arabic text Tanzil project se liya gaya hai (CC-BY 3.0 licence — text baghair tabdeeli ke, credit aur link ke sath).

Urdu tarjuma Kanz-ul-Iman ka hai aur English tarjuma Pickthall ka hai — dono public-domain kaam hain, mutarjim ke naam ke sath.

Namaz timings Karachi method (University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi — Fajr aur Isha 18°) par isi page par calculate hoti hain. Apni masjid ke timetable se 1–3 minute ka farq normal hai — method aur rounding ka farq hota hai.

Hijri tareekhein tabular (hisabi) hain — maqami chand dekhne ki tareekh se taqreeban 1 din ka farq ho sakta hai.

Hadith sirf us waqt shaya ki jati hai jab kitab, number aur darja (grade) maloom ho.

How this site is kept honest

Yahan koi jaali counter, jaali review ya khaali page nahi hai. Jo cheez maujood hai, wo poori hai; jo abhi nahi hai, wo dikhayi nahi jati. Agar kisi page par ghalti nazar aaye — waqt, tareekh, tarjuma ya hawala — to barah-e-karam Contact page se humein batayein, taake use durust kiya ja sake.

Method & honesty

Namaz times are calculated on-page (Karachi method, 18°/18°). Hijri dates are tabular — the local moon-sighting date can differ by about 1 day. Hadith are published only with book, number and grade.
