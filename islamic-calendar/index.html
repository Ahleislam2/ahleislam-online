Sunni Jantri

Mashhoor Urs, Khatam aur Din

Ye Sunni Urs chronologies se li gayi mashhoor tareekhen hain. Urs ki tareekh Hijri month/day se fixed hai; Gregorian tareekh tabular hisaab se hai, chand dekhne par 1 din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.

Har Mahine — Khatam ke Din

Chatti Sharif — har mahine ki 6 tareekh: Khwaja Garib Nawaz ki yaad me — Quran, Zikr, Fatiha, Isal-e-Sawab.
Gyarvi Sharif — har mahine ki 11 tareekh: Ghaus-e-Azam ki yaad me — Quran, Zikr, Fatiha, Isal-e-Sawab.
Saalana bara Urs: Chatti Sharif 6 Rajab (Ajmer Sharif), Gyarvi Sharif 11 Rabi al-Thani (Baghdad Sharif).

Saal Bhar ke Din — Hijri Tarteeb se

Note: Kuch buzurgon ki Urs tareekh mukhtalif lists me 1-2 din alag bhi milti hai (jaise Data Sahib 18-20 Safar). Is liye range wale Urs ko poore dinon ke tor par dikhaya gaya hai. Shab-e-Qadr ki exact raat maloom nahi — aakhri taaq raaton me talash ki jati hai, 27 ko aam tor par zyada zikr kiya jata hai.
