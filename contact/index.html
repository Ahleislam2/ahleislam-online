What to write about

Durusti (corrections): namaz ke auqaat, Hijri tareekh, tarjuma ya hadith ke hawale me koi ghalti nazar aaye to zaroor batayein.

Content ki ghalti: kisi page par tooti hui link, ghalat lafz ya ghalat maloomat ho to nishandahi karein.

Tajweez (suggestions): koi naya tool, topic ya behtari ki rai ho to likh bhejein — har tajweez par ghaur kiya jata hai, lekin har tajweez par amal ki zamanat nahi hai.

Method & honesty

Namaz times are calculated on-page (Karachi method, 18°/18°). Hijri dates are tabular — the local moon-sighting date can differ by about 1 day. Hadith are published only with book, number and grade.
