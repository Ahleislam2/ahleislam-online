About Gojra Namaz Timings

Gojra — Toba Tek Singh zila ka sheher, apni zarayi mandi ke liye jana jata hai. Yahan namaz ke auqaat Karachi method se nikale jate hain. Is page par Gojra (Punjab, Pakistan) ke namaz auqaat University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi ke method se nikale jate hain — Fajr aur Isha 18 degree par, Asr Hanafi. Auqaat har roz khud-ba-khud update hote hain; koi fixed timetable nahi hai.

Sehri ka waqt Fajr se shuru hota hai aur Iftar Maghrib ke waqt hota hai. Ramadan me bhi yahi auqaat Sehri aur Iftar ke liye istemal hote hain.

FAQs — Gojra Namaz Timings

Gojra me namaz timings kis method se nikali jati hain?

University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi ke method se — Fajr aur Isha 18° par. Auqaat isi page par Gojra ke asal coordinates se hisaab kiye jate hain, koi bahari API nahi.

Asr ka waqt Hanafi hai ya Shafi?

Hanafi. Ye site Ahl-e-Sunnat wal Jamaat (Hanafi) ke mutabiq hai — Asr ka waqt Hanafi tareeqe se nikala jata hai.

Masjid ke timetable se 1–3 minute ka farq kyun hota hai?

Har masjid apne hisaab se ihtiyat ke chand minute jodti ya ghata deti hai, aur method/rounding bhi mukhtalif ho sakti hai. 1–3 minute ka farq aam baat hai.

Hijri date 1 din aage-peeche kyun ho sakti hai?

Is page ki Hijri tareekh tabular (hisaabi) hai. Pakistan me elan-shuda tareekh Ruet-e-Hilal Committee ke chand dekhne par hoti hai, jo 1 din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.
